# Contact lenses

Contacts that bother you by the end of the day are almost never the wrong brand: they are the wrong measurement. Fitting them means measuring your eye, not just your prescription.

### What to expect

After the eye exam, a fitting adds 20 to 30 minutes.

1. **What you will do with them.** Sport, work, occasional use, all day. It changes the recommendation.
2. **Measuring the surface of the eye.** Curvature and diameter decide which lens sits well.
3. **Checking your tear film.** An eye that dries out tolerates a fast-evaporating lens poorly. Worth knowing beforehand.
4. **Trying a trial lens** and watching how it moves and centers.
5. **Teaching you to insert and remove them,** if this is your first time. Nobody leaves without knowing how.
6. **A follow-up check** to confirm they are still comfortable after a few days.

### Who it is for

* You have never worn them and want to try.
* The ones you have bother you, dry out or shift.
* You play sports and glasses get in the way.
* You want to alternate between contacts and glasses.

### Frequently asked questions

**Can I sleep in them?**
It depends on the lens type, and most are not made for it. We tell you exactly how many hours yours allows; exceeding that is the most common cause of problems.

**Can I use my glasses prescription?**
Not directly. A contact lens rests on the eye while glasses sit a few millimeters away, so the values change. A fitting is needed.

**What if I wear contacts and also want glasses?**
That is what we recommend: keep a pair of glasses for the days your eyes need a rest.
