# Comprehensive eye exam

A complete exam does two things at once: it determines your exact prescription and it checks the condition of your eyes. Those are different things, and both matter.

### What to expect

The visit takes between 45 minutes and an hour.

1. **Your vision history.** What you have noticed, what you do for work, how many hours you spend on screens, whether anyone in your family has an eye condition.
2. **How much you see.** With a chart, at distance and up close.
3. **Your prescription.** Measured precisely and refined by trying lenses until the image is sharp and comfortable.
4. **How your two eyes work together.** Focusing, coordination and tracking. This is what decides whether reading tires you.
5. **Eye health.** A check of the structures of the eye, inside and out.
6. **The conversation.** We explain what we found, in the language you prefer.

### Who it is for

* It has been more than a year since your last exam, or you cannot remember when it was.
* Your vision is blurry at distance, up close, or both.
* You get headaches when reading or at the end of the workday.
* You are having trouble seeing at night or while driving.
* You see fine but you have diabetes, or someone in your family has glaucoma.

That last point is not a mistake. Several eye conditions progress without discomfort and without taking away vision until damage is already done. That is why the exam is worth having even when your vision feels perfect.

### Frequently asked questions

**Will my pupils be dilated?**
Depending on the case. If it is needed, your vision stays blurry for a couple of hours: bring sunglasses and plan not to drive. Ask when we call and we will plan around it.

**Do I leave with a prescription the same day?**
Yes. And if you want, you can choose frames during the same visit.

**Is it worth it if I only want to update my glasses?**
Yes, and that is the most common reason people come. Prescriptions shift over the years, and an old one tires your eyes without your noticing.
