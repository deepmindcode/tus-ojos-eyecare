# Professional Care for Your Family's Vision

At Tus Ojos Eyecare we provide personalized vision care for your whole family. Eye examinations are performed by accredited eye care professionals who assess each case individually.

Please contact us to confirm service availability at your preferred office. Once you submit an appointment request, our office calls you directly to arrange the day and time of your visit.

### Our services

* **Comprehensive eye exams:** complete assessments to determine your prescription and check overall eye wellness.
* **Pediatric eye care:** gentle, preventive vision assessments that support learning and childhood development.
* **Contact lens fittings:** tailored evaluations for comfort, clarity, and the right fit for your lifestyle.
* **Optical and frames:** expert guidance with a wide selection of frames and our own optical lab.
* **Prescription sunglasses:** UV protection combined with your prescription for everyday use.
* **Diabetic eye wellness screenings:** preventive assessments coordinated with your broader health care.
* **Dry eye evaluation and relief:** evaluations to identify sources of irritation and build a daily comfort plan.
* **Red eye and irritation consultations:** prompt assessment for persistent discomfort or redness.
* **Advanced diagnostic eye testing:** technology that helps detect early signs of conditions affecting your eyesight.

Available services may vary by office. Please check with your preferred location before your visit.
