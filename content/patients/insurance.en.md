# Insurance and payment options

We accept most of the popular vision plans. One thing is worth knowing before your visit: **many plans do not cover every treatment or every service**, and what they cover differs from plan to plan.

That is why we ask you to check directly with the office where you will be seen. It is the only way to know for certain what your plan covers in your case.

### How we confirm it

When you submit an appointment request, our team calls you. On that call we review your plan details and tell you what is covered and what is not, before you come in. No surprises at the front desk.

* We work with most of the better-known vision plans.
* Accessible options are available for patients without insurance.
* We accept cash, credit and debit cards, and HSA and FSA accounts.

Request your appointment online and use the call to ask about your coverage.
